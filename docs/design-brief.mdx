## 1. The brand

### 1.1 Who Koko Atelier is

Koko Atelier is a Lahore fashion label for women and men: pret, festive and luxury wear (kurtas, anarkalis, co-ords, lehengas, sarees, men's kurta sets), with craft rooted in South Asian technique: hand embroidery, zardozi and resham, mirror-work, appliqué, scalloped lace, digital prints.

From the website's About page:

> "At Koko Atelier, we craft fashion that speaks to the modern woman — elegant, confident, and unapologetically herself."
>
> "We believe fashion should feel as good as it looks."
>
> "Koko Atelier is here to dress your story with style and soul."

Positioning: **everyday luxury** ("Founded with the vision of redefining everyday luxury"). Heritage craft, modern cut, quiet confidence.

### 1.2 Brand personality

| Is | Is not |
| --- | --- |
| Elegant, considered | Flashy, loud |
| Warm, personal | Corporate, cold |
| Poetic (Urdu names, imagery) | Gimmicky |
| Confident and calm | Pushy, salesy |
| Crafted, detailed | Generic, mass-market |

### 1.3 Name and wordmark

- Written **Koko Atelier** in running text (title case). **KOKO ATELIER** in capitals only inside the logo, footers and small eyebrow labels.
- The app's working name is **Koko Atelier CRM**. Anything a customer or reseller receives (receipts, statements, product sheets) says **Koko Atelier**, never "CRM".
- Legal name on financial statements: **Koko Atelier (Private) Limited**.

### 1.5 Voice and tone

The website speaks in short, sensory, confident lines. Product copy pairs an Urdu name with its English meaning and describes the craft:

> "Shab: An all-black silk kurta and trouser set that speaks quietly."
> "Gulnaar: A wine-deep anarkali built for movement."
> "Shab-e-Siyah: Dark Night." · "Zard Chandni: Golden Moonlight." · "Junoon: Obsession."

**In the CRM** the voice is the same brand speaking to its own team: calm, plain and helpful.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Plain English, sentence case: "Record a payment" | Title Case Everywhere, jargon ("Submit Transaction") |
| Say what happens: "Posted to the books", "Moved to Paid" | Vague: "Success!" |
| Errors say what to do: "Choose the sales channel" | "Error 400: invalid channel" |
| Name things the way the studio does: "Back to studio", "Sell this piece", "Studio claims", "Sticker-note words" | Accounting or database terms on non-finance screens |
| Keep product names exactly as the website spells them (Shab-e-Siyah, Gul-e-Mehtab, Sunehri) | Re-spell or translate product names |
| Use "Rs" or "PKR" consistently per surface (see 2.6) | Mix "Rs.", "PKR", "₨" on one screen |

Accounting terms are allowed inside Finance, where the reader is an owner or accountant; even there, prefer "what is owed" over "receivable" in helper text.

### 1.6 Imagery

- **The outfits lead.** Product photography from the website (Shopify CDN) is the hero of the sign-in lookbook, product pages, order items, receipts and the reseller product sheet.
- Show photos uncropped at fashion proportions (portrait, roughly 3:4); never put text over the garment.
- Backgrounds around photos stay neutral (porcelain or white) so fabric colours read true. That is why the app is white-based: an earlier cream theme ("Minimal Yet Warm") was dropped on 18 November 2026 in favour of white.
- No stock photography, no illustrations of people, no emoji in the interface.

## 2. Design system: "Porcelain & Wine"

Chosen by the owner on 18 November 2026 from four white-based options: a white, luxury fashion feel that lets the outfit photos lead, with animation that feels considered.

### 2.1 Colour

**Core palette**

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Porcelain (canvas) | `#FBFAFB` | Page background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, menus, dialogs |
| Blush (surface-accent) | `#F6F1F3` | Table headers, quiet highlights |
| Ink | `#1C1A1F` | Primary text, sidebar background |
| Raised ink | `#2C2830` | Active sidebar item background |
| Grey | `#7A7480` | Secondary text, labels |
| Faint | `#A39DA8` | Hints, placeholders |
| Line | `#ECE8EB` | Borders, row dividers |
| **Wine** (brand primary) | `#7B2D45` | Primary buttons, links, active text, focus rings |
| **Champagne** (highlight) | `#C9A96E` | Active menu marker on dark, gold rules, "At Store" / "New" chips |
| Alert | `#D9473A` | Errors, destructive actions |

**Wine scale** (Tailwind `plum` / `brand`): 50 `#FBF4F6` · 100 `#F6E6EB` · 200 `#EBC9D3` · 300 `#D99DB0` · 400 `#A9506B` · **500 `#7B2D45`** · 600 `#6A263B` · 700 `#5B2034` · 800 `#451828`.

**Champagne scale** (Tailwind `gold`): 50 `#FBF8F2` · 100 `#F5EEDF` · 200 `#EBDDC0` · 300 `#DDC79C` · **400 `#C9A96E`** · 500 `#B08F55`.

**Semantic colours** (Tailwind hues are remapped in `tailwind.config.js` so every chip stays inside the palette):

| Meaning | Scale | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Done, paid, posted, positive money | Sage (`emerald` / `green`) `#22A55A` | Paid, Posted, balance cleared |
| Pending, in progress, needs attention | Warm (`amber` / `orange`) `#E39B2B` | In Tailoring, Payment Pending, Sold (awaiting payment) |
| Brand state | Wine (`plum`, `violet`) | Ready |
| Information | Slate (`sky` / `blue`) `#7A7480` | Out for Delivery |
| Neutral / closed | Stone grey | Returned |
| Error, reversed, rejected | Alert (`rose` / `red`) `#D9473A` | Cancelled, Reversed, Rejected |
| New / at store | Champagne | New, At Store |

**Dark mode** (class `dark`, toggled in the top bar; light is the default, stored as `koko-theme-v2`):

| Token | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Canvas | `#141216` |
| Surface | `#1E1B21` |
| Line | `#2F2A33` |
| Ink (text) | `#F4EFF2` |
| Muted | `#A59EAA` |
| Accent (readable wine) | `#D98AA2` |

Always use tokens (`bg-canvas`, `bg-surface`, `text-ink`, `text-muted`, `border-line`, `text-accent`, `bg-brand-primary`) so both themes work. Never hard-code hex values in components.

### 2.2 Typography

- **Inter** for everything (UI, headings, numbers). Hierarchy comes from size, weight and letter-spacing, not from mixing typefaces.
- Numbers use tabular figures (`tabular-nums`) so amounts line up in tables.

| Style | Spec | Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page title | 24 px on phones, 28 px from 640 px, ink | `h1` in `PageHeader` (`text-2xl sm:text-[1.75rem]`) |
| Eyebrow (above title) | 11 px, semibold, uppercase, tracking 0.2em, grey | `.eyebrow` |
| Section title | 11 px, semibold, uppercase, tracking 0.18em, grey | `.section-title` |
| Form label / table header | 10.5 px, semibold, uppercase, tracking-wider | `.label`, `.th` |
| Body | 14 px regular, ink | `text-sm` |
| Secondary | 12 px, grey | `text-xs text-muted` |
| Big figure | 24–28 px semibold | `StatCard`, balance due |

Uppercase is only for small labels (eyebrows, section titles, table headers). Never set body text or buttons in capitals.

### 2.3 Space, shape, depth

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Base spacing | 4 px grid; page padding 16 px (phone), 24 px (tablet), 32 px (desktop); content max width 1152 px (`max-w-6xl`) |
| Corner radius | Inputs and buttons 8 px (`rounded-lg`); cards 12 px (`rounded-xl`); dialogs and large cards 20 px (`rounded-2xl`); chips fully round |
| Borders | 1 px `line`; tables divide rows with `line` |
| Shadows | `soft` for cards; `lift` for dialogs and popovers; never heavy |
| Accent rule | `.gold-rule`: a 1 px wine-to-champagne gradient under stat cards and section headers |

### 2.4 Motion

- framer-motion, short and soft: 0.2–0.3 s, ease-out. Lists stagger in; dialogs rise 40 px and fade; status changes animate the chip.
- Buttons press to 98% scale. Numbers in stat cards count up once.
- Motion never blocks work: no animation longer than 0.8 s, nothing loops except loading skeletons and the sync spinner.

### 2.5 Iconography

- lucide-react outline icons, 14–16 px in text and buttons, 24 px in empty states, stroke default.
- One icon per page header (in a soft wine tile) and per menu item.

### 2.6 Numbers, money and dates

| Item | Format | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retail money | `PKR 12,000` (no decimals) | CRM screens, receipts |
| Finance money | `Rs 23,200.00` (two decimals); negatives in brackets `(1,500.00)` | Finance screens and reports |
| Reseller money | `AED 1,050.00` alongside PKR | Reseller screens |
| Website (reference) | `Rs.14,250.00`, compare-at struck through | kokoatelier.com |
| Dates | `23 Nov 2026` (screens); `23 November 2026` (documents) | Pakistan date (Asia/Karachi) |
| Order numbers | `SO-000123`; website `#1151` | |
| Entry numbers | `JV-2627-000001` (type, fiscal year, number) | Finance |
| Barcodes | `KOATLR-011-S` (prefix, design number, size) | Reseller |

Recommendation: align the retail format on screens with the website over time (Rs with thousands separators) so the brand reads the same everywhere.

## 3. Components

All in `src/ui/kit.jsx` and `src/index.css`; reuse before creating new ones.

| Component | Look and use |
| --- | --- |
| `PageHeader` | Eyebrow, title, subtitle, icon tile, actions on the right |
| `.card` | White surface, 1 px line, soft shadow, 12 px radius; content padding 20 px |
| `StatCard` | Label, big animated figure, optional sub-line, gold rule at the bottom; tones default / green / red / orange / plum |
| `Modal` | Centred dialog on desktop (sizes sm, md, lg, xl), bottom sheet on phones; title + subtitle, scrolling body, footer with actions right-aligned |
| `Field` + `.input` + `.label` | Uppercase label above, input with wine focus ring, hint or error below |
| `Segmented` | Pill tabs with a sliding white indicator (page tabs, filters) |
| Buttons | `.btn-primary` wine (one per view), `.btn-secondary` white outline, `.btn-ghost` text, `.btn-icon` square 32 px, `.btn-danger` alert, `.btn-gold` champagne (rare highlight) |
| Status chips | Rounded pill, tinted background, coloured dot + label (`StatusBadge`, `FinBadge`) |
| Tables | Blush header row with uppercase labels, row dividers, hover tint; money right-aligned and tabular |
| `EmptyState` | Soft wine icon circle, short title, one-line hint, optional action |
| `SkeletonRows` / `SkeletonCards` | Grey shimmer while loading; never a blank page |
| `ErrorBanner` | Rose box with retry |
| Toasts (sonner) | Bottom-right; short past-tense sentences ("Payment recorded") |

## 4. Layout and responsive behaviour

- **Shell:** dark ink sidebar (wordmark in white, sections in uppercase grey, active item in champagne on raised ink) and a white content area with a top bar (search, theme toggle).
- **Phone (under 640 px):** bottom tab bar plus "More" sheet; tables scroll horizontally inside their card; dialogs are bottom sheets; primary actions stay reachable with a thumb.
- **Grids:** stat cards 2 across on phone, 3–4 on desktop; detail pages two columns (content left, summary cards right) from 1024 px.
- **Density:** finance tables are denser (14 px rows); retail screens breathe more around photos.

## 5. Documents the CRM produces

| Document | Brand treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Order receipt PDF | Koko logo, wine and champagne accents (`C` palette in `generatePDF.js`), product photos, measurements, totals; customer-facing, says "Koko Atelier" |
| Reseller statement PDF | Month summary with AED and PKR; landscape A4; hides on-screen controls (`data-pdf-hide`) |
| Product sheet | Photos, names and barcodes for the reseller |
| Finance reports (PDF, Excel) | "Koko Atelier (Private) Limited", report title, period, wordmark top right |
| Courier booking Excel | Functional (Trax format); no branding |

## 6. Accessibility

- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (ink on porcelain, white on wine both pass); never grey text on blush for important information.
- Wine is too dark on the near-black sidebar, so the active item uses champagne there; in dark mode links use `#D98AA2`.
- Visible focus ring (wine, 2 px) on every interactive element; dialogs close with Escape.
- Status is never shown by colour alone: chips always carry the word.
- Touch targets at least 32 px (44 px for primary actions on phones).

## 7. Applying the brand to new work

1. Start from an existing screen in the same area and reuse its components.
2. One primary (wine) action per view; secondary actions in white.
3. Let photos breathe; never place UI over garments.
4. Write labels in the studio's words, sentence case, in the voice described in 1.5.
5. Check both light and dark themes and a 390 px phone width before shipping.

## 8. Open items

- The website's exact theme colours and typefaces could not be read from the storefront automatically. Confirm them from the Shopify theme settings (Online Store › Themes › Customize › Theme settings) and record them here if the CRM should match the website more closely.
- Decide whether customer-facing PDFs should switch from the legacy `kokoLogo.jpg` to the `public/brand/` wordmark.
