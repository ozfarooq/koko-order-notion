This document maps every screen and how people move through them. Flows are written as numbered steps; system actions (what the app does on its own) are in *italics*.

## 1. Entry and navigation

### 1.1 Sign-in

1. User opens the app. *If there is no valid session, the sign-in page opens.*
2. The sign-in page shows the Koko wordmark and a lookbook of photos from the three newest collections (public `/api/lookbook`).
3. User enters username and password.
   - *Wrong password: "Wrong username or password". After 5 failures the account is locked for 15 minutes.*
4. *On success the app opens the role's home page:*

| Role | Home page |
| --- | --- |
| Owner, Manager, Staff | `/dashboard` |
| Finance Manager | `/finance/reports` |
| Reseller | `/resellers/:id` (their own dashboard) |

5. Users can change their password from the sidebar footer; "Log out" ends the session.

## 3. Retail flows

### 3.1 Create an order (studio, WhatsApp, Instagram)

1. **New Order** (menu, or "Sell this piece" from Inventory).
2. **Customer:** type a name or phone. *The picker finds existing customers by name or the last 10 digits of the phone.* Pick one or keep typing to create a new customer.
3. **Channel** (required): Studio, WhatsApp, Instagram, Shopify (with website order number), Other. **Reference:** how the sale came in (Walk-in, Outstation, a person).
4. **Items:** search the catalogue (photo, collection, price). Set size and quantity; price defaults to the list price.
   - *If a matching studio piece exists (sample, return, damaged-for-sale), the line can be filled from it; damaged pieces are never picked automatically.*
5. **Order type:** Sale, or PR / gift (influencer handle; amount and advance forced to 0).
6. **Dates:** order date (today), delivery date.
7. **Delivery address and city** (defaults from the customer).
8. **Measurements** (optional, standard fields).
9. **Payment:** total amount, advance received, payment notes. *The advance becomes a payment row (method assumed bank, marked "check").*
10. **Save.** *Order number SO-000123 is assigned; the order opens; audit entry written; finance posts the advance as a customer advance.*

### 3.2 Import website orders (Shopify)

1. Orders › **Import from website**.
2. *The app lists website orders from the last 60 days that are not yet in the CRM.*
3. Tick the orders to bring in → **Import**.
4. *Each becomes an order with channel Shopify and its #number; customer matched or created.*

### 3.3 Work an order through production

```
New ──► In Tailoring ──► Ready ──► Payment Pending ──► Paid ──► Out for Delivery
  │                                                    │
  └──────────────► Cancelled                           └──► Returned
```

1. Change status from the order page (status menu) or by dragging on the **Status Board**.
2. *Status change is audited; `status_changed_on` set.*
3. **Paid:** *the advance is topped up to the total (a "final payment" row), `paid_on` = today, studio pieces on the order become Sold, finance posts the sale (Dr customer advances, Cr sales).*
4. **Out for Delivery:** *studio pieces on the order become Sold (Gifted for PR).*
5. **Returned:** *the sale is reversed in the accounts; studio pieces go back to the studio; the app offers to add the returned items to studio stock as "Customer return".*
6. **Cancelled:** *reserved studio pieces are freed; any advance shows under Finance › Sales & receipts as a refund due.*

### 3.4 Record a payment or refund

1. Order page › Payment card › **Record**.
2. Amount (defaults to the balance), date, paid into (Bank Alfalah, Cash, Trax COD, paid to Omer / Usman), reference, notes. Tick "This is a refund" for money going back.
3. **Save.** *Payment row added; advance = sum of payments; balance updates; finance posts the receipt (or refund).*
4. To correct a payment, tap it (change method, amount, date) or **Remove** it. *The accounting entry is reversed and reposted.*

### 3.5 Delivery and documents

1. Order page › **PDF**: branded receipt with photos, measurements, totals.
2. Orders list › select orders › **Courier Excel** for Trax booking.
3. Order page › Delivery card › **Trax tracking** (add or change).

### 3.6 Customers

1. **Customers** list: search by name, phone, city or email; sort by most recent order, total spend, balance due, number of orders or name.
2. Profile: contact, orders, totals, measurements; edit details.
3. **Merge** a duplicate into the right profile (orders move across).

## 4. Catalogue flows

1. **Catalogue › Sync from website**: *reads collections and products from kokoatelier.com, shows what is new or changed; owner applies.*
2. **Import CSV** for pieces not on the website.
3. Product page: gallery, tagline (editable), where the design is (stock, resellers), reseller codes.

## 5. Studio inventory flows

### 5.1 Add pieces

1. **Inventory › Add pieces** (or **Bulk add**): product, sizes and counts, kind (Stock / Sample), status (In Production, At Studio, At Shoot), source.
2. *One record per piece; every status change is logged in the piece history.*

### 5.2 Move and sell

1. Open a piece: move status (At Studio ↔ At Shoot, Archived, Damaged written off, Gifted with a "given to" note).
2. **Damaged-for-sale:** mark damaged with a note and an asking price; the piece stays sellable.
3. **Sell this piece:** *opens New Order with a line pinned to that piece.*

## 6. Reseller flows (Koko team)

### 6.1 Hand over stock

```
Studio pieces ──► Handover (date, commission %) ──► At Store at the reseller
                                                     │
                       monthly statement ──► Sold ───┤
                       payment received  ──► Paid ───┘
                       returned          ──► Returned ──► Back to studio (optional)
```

1. Reseller › **Handovers** › New handover: date, commission % (copied from the reseller), pieces (product, size, price in AED).
2. *The oldest At Studio piece of that product and size is used (FIFO); otherwise a newly made piece is recorded as With Reseller.*
3. *Designs new to this reseller are listed as needing a barcode once the shipment is approved.*

### 6.2 Barcodes

1. Reseller › **Settings** › Design codes: set prefix (KOATLR) and next number (16).
2. Type the reseller's confirmed code for a design, or give the next number. *Till barcode = code + size (KOATLR-011-S).*

### 6.3 Monthly statement

1. Reseller › **Statements** › **Enter sales**: pick outfits from the collection picker (photos), set sold date, discounts, alteration, invoice number. Or **Import sheet** (the reseller's Excel): *rows are matched to pieces by code and size; review then apply.*
2. *Pieces move to Sold; month summary updates; finance posts each sale at the AED rate of its sale date.*
3. **Download PDF** of the month's statement.

### 6.4 Payment from the reseller

1. Statements › **Payments** › Record payment: date, AED amount, PKR received, period (month), reference, difference reason, proof files; choose the sold pieces it pays.
2. *Pieces move to Paid; month shows paid; finance posts the receipt, clears what was owed and books the exchange difference.*

### 6.5 Returns

1. Stock › select At Store pieces › **Returned** (reason), or **Back to studio** (optionally damaged, with note and asking price).
2. *Returned pieces leave the reseller; Back to studio creates an At Studio piece (source Reseller return). A sale already posted is reversed.*
3. *Sold, Paid and returned-to-studio pieces are locked from edits.*

## 7. Reseller portal (Fashion Collage login)

1. Sign in → **their dashboard**: sales by month, what is due, payment-due banner.
2. **My Stock**: what is at the store, sold and paid.
3. **Statements**: month summaries and payments received (no Koko notes, references or proof files).
4. **Inventory report**, **Product sheet** (photos and codes), **Help**.
5. *Everything is view-only; no buttons that change data are shown.*

## 8. Finance flows (owner, Finance Manager)

### 8.1 Daily: expenses and receipts

1. **Expenses › Add expense** (phone friendly): amount, date, what for, category, paid from (bank, petty cash, Omer / Usman personally, not paid yet), supplier (or new), reference, collection, receipt photo. *Posted at once; a hint suggests a fixed-asset category at Rs 25,000 or more.*
2. Unpaid bill → later **Pay bill** (date, account, reference).
3. Correct or void → *reversal and repost.*

### 8.2 Inbox (receipts and bank screenshots)

```
Upload files ─► Read (Claude vision, or type in) ─► Classify ─► Review beside picture ─► Approve
                                                       │                                   │
                                            Duplicate / Needs attention         Expense, staff payment
                                            / Parked (money coming in)          or funds added posted;
                                                                                file renamed and filed
```

1. **Inbox › Add receipts & screenshots** (many at once). *Same file twice is caught.*
2. *Each file is read automatically (when the API key is set), or the user taps "Type the details in".*
3. *Rules classify:* director → Koko = funds added; Koko → staff = staff payment split by sticker words (Salary, Market, Advance); Koko → anyone else or a shop receipt = expense; money coming in = parked for F3 matching.
4. Open an item: picture on the left, proposed entry on the right; fix anything, then **Approve & post**. Or **Park**, **Reject**.
5. **Approve sure ones**: posts all ready items read with 85%+ confidence.

### 8.3 Weekly: studio claim and payroll

1. **Studio claims › New claim**: who, week ending (Saturday), items with category and amount, receipt photos → Submit. Owner **Approves** (*booked*) or rejects.
2. **Payroll › Start this week** (week ending Saturday). *Everyone on a weekly salary is filled in at full attendance.*
3. Adjust days present, pay, advance recovered; split hours by job; add custom-work and commission income → **Post week**. *Wages booked; a week over a month end is split by working days.*
4. **Payments to staff › Pay someone**: tick the weeks and approved claims the transfer covers, add an advance, reference, screenshot → **Record**. *One bank payment settles salary and claim together.*

### 8.4 Sales & receipts

1. **Posting status:** *orders, payments and reseller statements post on their own.* Anything waiting (missing exchange rate, closed month) is listed with **Retry**. **Check everything now** re-compares all orders and reseller statements with the books.
2. **Missing rates:** type the AED rate for each listed date → *waiting sales post.*
3. **Refunds due:** cancelled or returned orders still holding an advance → **Refund** (record on the order) or **Keep** (owner, with reason).
4. **Receivables:** reseller ageing (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days), open customer orders, advances check.
5. **Exchange rates:** add or delete daily rates.

### 8.5 Month end and reports

1. **Reports:** trial balance, P&L (compare previous / last year), balance sheet, cash flows, changes in equity; Excel and PDF export; click a figure to open its ledger.
2. **Ledgers:** any account, running balance, quick picks (bank, petty cash, directors, customer advances).
3. **Journals:** manual journals (control accounts blocked), drafts, post, reverse with reason.
4. **Finance settings:** chart of accounts, periods (Open → Closed → Locked; owner can post into Closed with a reason), opening balances (grid or Excel template), company settings.

## 9. Admin flows

1. **Users › Add user**: name, username, password, role; for a reseller login, link the reseller. Deactivate users who leave.
2. **Add reseller**: name, city, country, currency, commission %, barcode prefix.

## 10. System behaviours users should know

| Event | What the app does |
| --- | --- |
| Any save | Audit log entry (who, what, when) |
| Advance changed on the order form | A payment row is recorded (method to confirm) |
| Order or reseller change | Finance re-checks that order or reseller and posts the difference; never blocks the save |
| Delete an order already in the accounts | Refused: cancel it instead |
| Reseller sale in a closed month | Posted into the next open month, sale month in the memo |
| File uploaded twice | Recognised by fingerprint, stored once |
| Session idle 14 days | Signed out |
