## 1. Purpose and how to use this document

This document specifies a finance module for the Koko Atelier CRM. It covers eight capabilities requested by the owner: Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, Ledgers, Trial Balance, Manufacturing Cost (costing per design, cost per piece, profit per order and per collection), Pakistan tax reporting, Expense Tracking and Bank Reconciliation.

It is written to be handed to Claude Code in VS Code at the repo root, one phase at a time. Each phase in Section 12 is self-contained, lists its migrations, routes, screens and a "Done when" check, and ends with a review gate before the next phase starts.

- **Sections 2 to 4** explain the existing system and the business so the code fits what is already there.
- **Sections 5 to 8** are the functional requirements for each capability. **Section 9** covers IFRS compliance.
- **Sections 10 and 11** are the data model and the API / screen plan.
- **Section 12** is the phased plan. **Section 15** is the prompt to paste into Claude Code.

> **Tax is a later phase, switched off at launch.** Koko does not tax orders today but plans to. The full Pakistan tax design (Section 8) is kept and built as the last phase, F7. From Phase F0 the ledger carries the hooks it needs (a `tax_enabled` setting, a `tax_code` on every line and inactive tax accounts), so tax is switched on later without rebuilding anything. While `tax_enabled` is false, prices are final revenue and courier or bank deductions are costs.

> **Tax disclaimer.** Pakistan tax rates and rules in this document reflect Finance Act 2026 as publicly summarised in November 2026. They are inputs to a configurable rate table, not hard-coded logic. Every rate and treatment must be confirmed by Koko's tax advisor before returns are filed from this system.

## 3. Business context for finance

The design is driven by how Koko actually earns and spends money. Claude Code must treat these as requirements, not background.

- **Stitch-to-order retail.** A customer order takes an advance, is tailored, then delivered. The balance arrives at delivery: cash in studio, bank transfer, or cash on delivery (COD) collected by Trax courier.
- **Trax COD settlements.** Trax pays out by invoice: collections less delivery charges, less taxes withheld, plus an IBFT charge of Rs 100 per invoice. A negative net disbursement means a charge-only entry; a positive one means collection and charge entries. Amount Received values must sum to the invoice total. This logic already exists in Omer's CSV process and must be reproduced.
- **Bank.** Bank Alfalah (BAF) PKR is the only bank account. Reseller money from Fashion Collage Dubai also arrives in this account, already converted to PKR. A studio petty cash float also exists. No AED account and no mobile wallets.
- **Resellers (consignment).** Stock is made in advance and handed to Fashion Collage Dubai (AED) and FPL (Pakistan). Revenue happens when the reseller sells, not at handover. The reseller deducts commission and alteration/laundry charges, then remits. The PKR that reaches BAF can differ from the AED statement because of bank charges and exchange rate.
- **PR orders.** Outfits sent to influencers are stitched and delivered but never revenue. Their cost is a marketing expense.
- **Studio labour.** Abdul Rahman (main tailor), Jahangir (embroidery, Rs 15,000/week) and Master Iftikhar (cutting master) are on fixed weekly salaries, Monday to Saturday. Tailors and embroidery work 10 hours/day, the master 4 hours/day. Hours and job entries come from a handwritten Saturday worksheet.
- **Per-piece tailor.** Manaf works per piece, is not paid by Koko, and the studio earns a commission on his output through Master Iftikhar. This is commission income.
- **Custom work income.** When salaried staff do custom work outside normal duties, the amount is noted and the money comes to Omer, who handles all accounts. It is recorded straight into the bank or cash as studio revenue, not extra wages.
- **Weekly studio expense claim.** Abdul Rahman submits a weekly claim (water, fabric top-ups, electrician / carpenter labour etc.) for reimbursement.
- **Directors pay personally.** Omer and Usman sometimes pay business costs from personal funds or receive business money personally. Director current accounts are required so this is never lost.
- **Payment proof = phone screenshots.** Most money movements are bank-app screenshots (Bank Alfalah alfa app, Askari etc.) with a sticker note added on the image, e.g. "Funds added OmerF 31700/-" (Omer moving personal money into the Koko account) or "Nov 26 Week 3, Tailor Salary 17500/-, Market 5700/-, Final Payment 23200/-" (one transfer paying the main tailor's weekly salary plus his market expense claim). These screenshots, together with paper receipts, are the source to build the year's data from 1 July 2026.
- **Fabric is bought in bulk at shoot time.** For each collection the fabric for the shoot samples is bought in bulk (by yard, per colour), so there is leftover fabric in stock that later customer and reseller orders use.
- **Weekly stitching consumables.** During the week the studio buys small items (thread, pins, needles, chalk, interlining, etc.), usually through the main tailor's weekly market claim.
- **Standard cost is set at the shoot.** When a collection is shot, a costing sheet fixes each outfit's standard cost (fabric yards x rate, stitching, extras such as lace, dye, tassels, buttons) and the price (Section 7.4.4).
- **Deliveries.** Mostly Trax (with COD). Some orders go directly by Uber; the ride cost is a delivery expense of that order and any cash the rider brings back is a cash receipt.
- **Lean made-to-order model** after an earlier restructuring. Fixed costs are low, so per-order and per-collection margin is the key management number.

## 4. Analysis and design principles

### 4.1 Key decision: a real double-entry ledger underneath everything

All eight requested reports are views over one general ledger. If the ledger is right, the Trial Balance, P&amp;L and Balance Sheet are queries, not separate features. So the module is built as:

1. **A double-entry core**: chart of accounts, journal entries, journal lines, fiscal periods.
2. **Sub-ledgers that post automatically**: orders, reseller sales and payments, expenses, payroll, material purchases, inventory moves, bank lines. Users work in familiar screens; the system writes the journals.
3. **Reports** that read only from posted journal lines.

### 4.2 Principles Claude Code must follow

| # | Principle | Implementation rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Balanced always | A posted entry must have total debit = total credit (to the paisa). Enforced by a deferred constraint trigger in Postgres, not only in JS |
| P2 | Immutable once posted | Posted entries are never edited or deleted. Corrections are reversal entries (one click "Reverse") plus a new entry |
| P3 | Idempotent auto-posting | Every system entry carries `(source_type, source_id, source_event)` with a unique index, so re-running a posting or backfill never doubles it |
| P4 | Periods and locking | Monthly periods inside fiscal years (Jul to Jun). Closed periods reject new postings except by owner with a reason; locked periods reject all |
| P5 | PKR base, multi-currency lines | Every line stores PKR amount; foreign lines also store currency, foreign amount and rate. Reports are in PKR |
| P6 | Accrual basis, IFRS recognition | Revenue when control passes to the customer (delivery) or when a reseller sells to its customer (IFRS 15); costs when incurred. Cash collection is tracked separately and never drives revenue |
| P7 | Tax is switchable and rules are data | `fin_settings.tax_enabled` (default false) and `tax_enabled_from` date decide whether tax lines are posted. Rates live in an effective-dated `tax_rates` table editable by owner. No tax percentage appears in code. Entries dated before `tax_enabled_from` are never re-taxed |
| P8 | Money as NUMERIC | `NUMERIC(14,2)` in SQL; in JS use integer paisa or string decimals, never floating-point sums |
| P9 | Traceability | Every report figure drills down: report line -&gt; account ledger -&gt; journal entry -&gt; source document (order, expense, payment, statement line) |
| P10 | Do not break existing flows | Order, reseller and inventory screens keep working when finance is off. Posting failures are logged and shown in a "Posting exceptions" queue; they never block an order save |
| P11 | Framework-aware reporting | One ledger, recognition per IFRS; statements presented under the framework set in `fin_settings.reporting_framework` (Section 9) |

### 4.3 Gaps and risks found in the current data

- **Payments are not individual records.** Orders hold a single `advance_paid_pkr` number; there is no table of each customer payment (date, method, bank). Finance needs one, so Phase F3 adds `customer_payments` and migrates existing advances as opening records.
- **Revenue timing (decided, adjusted for IFRS).** The owner marks an order Paid when funds reach the account. IFRS 15 recognises revenue when the customer receives the outfit, not when cash arrives, so the module adds a `delivered_on` date to orders: set at studio handover, from Trax delivery status, or by hand. Revenue posts on `delivered_on`; Paid posts the cash receipt. For most studio and transfer orders both happen the same day. If `delivered_on` is missing when an order reaches Paid, the Paid date is used and the order is flagged.
- **Cancelled orders with advances (decided).** The advance is refunded. Cancelling an order with an advance opens a Refund step (amount, date, account) that posts the refund. A rare "keep advance" option stays available to the owner only, with a reason.
- **Tax starts later.** Koko does not tax orders today. Posting rules must produce correct entries in both modes: tax off (full price is revenue, courier deductions are an expense) and tax on (output tax separated from price, deductions held as tax credits). See Section 8 and Open Questions.
- **Costs are not captured anywhere yet.** No materials, purchases or BOMs exist. Costing (Phase F5) starts with standard costs per design and adds actuals later.
- **History before go-live.** Backfilling every past transaction is costly. Recommended: go live from 1 July 2026 (start of FY 2026-27) with opening balances at 30 June 2026, then backfill July to go-live from CRM data.

## 6. Functional requirements: core accounting

### 6.1 Journal entries

- Entry number format `JV-2627-000123` (fiscal year 2026-27, running number per year). System entries use prefixes by source: `SI` sales, `RC` receipt, `EX` expense, `PY` payroll, `PU` purchase, `IN` inventory, `BK` bank, `TX` tax, `CL` closing.
- Fields: date, period, memo, source (type, id, event), status (Draft / Posted / Reversed), reversal link, created by, posted by, posted at.
- Lines: account, debit or credit (exactly one non-zero), party (customer / reseller / supplier / staff / director / courier), currency, foreign amount, FX rate, and analysis tags: `order_id`, `collection_id`, `product_id`, `consignment_item_id`, `tax_code`, free-text description.
- Manual journal screen: grid entry with running debit/credit totals; Post button disabled until balanced; attach files.
- Reverse: creates a mirror entry dated today (or a chosen date in an open period) and marks the original Reversed.
- Recurring journals (e.g. monthly depreciation, rent accrual): template + "generate for period".

### 6.2 Fiscal years and periods

- Fiscal year Jul to Jun, labelled both `FY 2026-27` and `Tax Year 2027` (FBR naming).
- Monthly periods with status Open / Closed / Locked. Owner closes; closing runs checks (Section 6.7).
- Year-end close: zero income and expense accounts into Retained Earnings with a `CL` entry; opens next year.

### 6.3 Ledgers

Ledger screens all share one component: date range, opening balance, lines with running balance, closing balance, drill-down to entry and source, XLSX/PDF export.

| Ledger | Filter | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General ledger (any account) | Account, date range, tags | Core view. Also "account statement" PDF |
| Customer ledger | Customer | Advances, invoices on delivery, receipts, refunds. Shown as a tab on the existing Customer Profile page |
| Reseller ledger | Reseller, currency | Sales per statement month, commission, alteration, payments, FX difference. Shown in reseller section next to Statements |
| Supplier ledger | Supplier | Bills, payments, WHT deducted |
| Staff ledger | Staff member | Weekly salary, advances to staff, expense claims, reimbursements |
| Director current accounts | Omer / Usman | Money in and out personally; balance owed to / by each director |
| Bank and cash books | Bank / cash account | With reconciliation status per line |
| Tax ledgers | Tax code | Output tax, input tax, WHT suffered, WHT deducted |

Ageing reports for receivables (customers, resellers) and payables (suppliers, staff claims) in buckets 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days.

### 6.4 Trial Balance

- As at any date or for a period: account code, name, opening, period debit, period credit, closing; debit and credit columns must total equal, shown with a green "Balanced" or red difference banner.
- Options: hide zero-balance accounts, group by account type, compare with previous period.
- Drill-down from any row to the account ledger.

### 6.5 Profit and Loss

- Layout: Revenue (by channel) less returns = Net revenue; Cost of sales (materials, labour, outsourced, overhead, write-offs, variance) = **Gross profit and margin %**; Operating expenses by group = **Operating profit**; Other income/expense, FX; **Profit before tax**; Tax; **Profit after tax**.
- Periods: month, quarter, fiscal year to date, custom. Comparison columns: previous period, same period last year, and budget (optional, later).
- Segment filters using line tags: by channel, by collection, by reseller. "P&amp;L by collection" is a column-per-collection view.
- Until costing is live (Phase F5) the report shows a banner: "Cost of sales is incomplete before costing is enabled".

### 6.6 Balance Sheet

- As at any date, in the Companies Act style: Non-current assets; Current assets (inventories, receivables, tax refundable, cash and bank); Equity (share capital, retained earnings, current-year profit computed live); Non-current and current liabilities (payables, customer advances, taxes payable, director accounts).

### 7.4 Manufacturing cost (costing)

Goal: know the cost of every design, every piece, every order and every collection, and therefore real profit. Two layers, built in order:

#### Layer 1: Standard cost per design (bill of materials)

- **Materials master**: code, name, category (fabric, lining, trims, laces, buttons, embroidery thread, dye, packaging, label), unit (metre, yard, piece, gram, cone), current average cost, supplier, colour/shade notes.
- **Material purchases**: supplier bill with lines (material, quantity, unit price); updates weighted average cost and stock on hand.
- **Bulk fabric lots**: each fabric bought for a collection is a material lot named the way the costing sheet names it ("Notebook ref": Brown, Zinc, Red, Black velvet, Blue velvet, Ivory velvet), with yards bought, rate per yard and collection tag. Shoot samples draw yards from the lot; the **leftover yards stay in stock** and are drawn by later orders and reseller batches at the lot's average cost. A new purchase of the same fabric at a new rate updates the average. The Inventory page shows yards left per lot and how many more outfits that covers (yards left / yards per outfit). Monthly or quarterly fabric count posts any difference; small unusable remnants are written down (IAS 2 NRV).
- **Stitching consumables** (thread, pins, needles, chalk, interlining) are not tracked by quantity. They are entered as market-claim or expense lines with category Consumables (5410). At month-end they are spread over the pieces stitched that month (consumables / pieces) and absorbed into WIP and finished goods, so every piece carries its share. The BOM also holds a **standard consumables allowance per piece** (owner setting, e.g. Rs 150) so the cost card is complete; the monthly difference goes to costing variance.
- **BOM per product (design)**: versioned with effective date. Lines of four kinds:
  - **Material**: material, quantity per piece, wastage % (fabric often 5-10%), size factor (XS 0.9 ... XL 1.15, configurable) so large sizes consume more fabric.
  - **Stitching / labour**: standard stitching cost per piece as on the shoot costing sheet (e.g. MOKA 3,000), optionally split into operations and hours.
  - **Outsourced**: dyeing, printing, machine embroidery, adda work: cost per piece.
  - **Overhead**: management order profit shows contribution before overheads by default (owner setting). Separately, for IFRS (IAS 2) the month-end inventory valuation always absorbs fixed production overhead (studio rent, studio electricity, cutting master salary) into WIP and finished goods based on normal capacity (Section 9.3).
- **Standard cost card** per design and size: materials, labour, outsourced, overhead, total, retail price, gross margin %, and the minimum price for a target margin. Printable PDF for Usman.

#### Layer 2: Actual cost per job

- A **production job** is either a customer order (stitch-to-order) or a stock batch (reseller stock, samples). Created automatically when an order moves to In Tailoring, or when a batch is recorded in Inventory.
- Actual (spent) cost = fabric yards actually issued x lot average rate + extras issued + labour allocated from weekly payroll (salary / pieces or hours) + outsourced bills (dye, lace work) tagged to the job + consumables per piece for the month (+ fixed overhead per piece where switched on or for IFRS stock value).
- **Standard vs actual report** per design and per collection: standard cost from the shoot sheet, actual spent cost, difference by element (fabric, stitching, extras, consumables) and the margin at the final price on both bases.
- If actuals are not recorded for a job, standard cost is used and flagged "standard". The difference between actual and standard posts to Costing variances 5600.
- **Cost per piece** for a batch = batch actual cost / pieces produced; stored on each `stock_piece` (`unit_cost_pkr`).

#### 7.4.4 Pricing (standard cost at the shoot, then actual)

The module reproduces the pricing method on the VÉLORA costing sheet and then keeps checking it against spent cost.

- **Pricing policy** (owner settings, per collection with defaults): marketing / packaging allowance per piece (VÉLORA: Rs 3,000), profit % on cost (20%), courier % (2%), rounding rule (round up to the next 500 for the "without" price; final price confirmed by the owner).
- **Koko method (default, matches the sheet)**: Unit cost = production cost + allowance. Price = Unit cost x (1 + profit % + courier %) = Unit cost x 1.22. Final price = owner-confirmed rounded price. The "without allowance" price and the difference are shown alongside, as on the sheet.
- **Check at final price**: margin = final price - unit cost - courier deduction actually taken (2% of the **selling price**, not of cost). Shown as % on cost and % of price, so the owner sees the real profit after Trax's cut.
- **Optional gross-up method** (setting): Price = Unit cost x (1 + profit %) / (1 - courier %). This recovers the 2% on the selling price exactly; offered for future collections, not forced.
- **Repricing alert**: when the actual spent cost of a design (from Layer 2) moves its margin at the final price below the target profit %, the design is flagged on the Costing page with the price the policy would now suggest.
- **Allowance check**: actual marketing and packaging spend tagged to the collection / pieces sold, against the Rs 3,000 allowance, so the owner sees whether the allowance covered the real spend.
- Price history is stored per product (standard cost snapshot, computed price, final price, approved by, date); the confirmed final price can be pushed to the CRM product price (`products.price_pkr`).

#### Intake: building the year from receipts and screenshots

Omer drops every receipt photo, bill PDF and bank-app screenshot for the year into the **Inbox** folder. The system turns them into draft entries for review, so the July 2026 to date data is built from the files instead of typed by hand.

1. **Scan**: "Sync inbox" button (and an optional daily scheduled run) lists new files in Inbox.
2. **Read**: each file is sent to the Claude API (vision) with a fixed extraction prompt (env `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`). It returns JSON: source app / bank, transaction reference number, date and time, amount, from account (name + last 4 digits only), to account (name + last 4), purpose field, and the **sticker note text** with any split lines (e.g. Tailor Salary 17,500 + Market 5,700 = 23,200), or for a paper receipt: shop, items, total.
3. **Classify**: rules first, then the model's suggestion: from Omer's personal account to Koko BAF = owner funding; from Koko to a staff member = salary / claim (by sticker words Salary, Market, Advance); from Koko to a supplier = supplier payment; into Koko from a customer = customer receipt (suggest matching order by amount, name and date); Collage remittance = reseller payment.
4. **Draft**: creates the draft entry or document (payroll payment, claim reimbursement, expense, owner funding, customer payment) with the file attached and a confidence score. Splits must add up to the amount, otherwise the item is marked "needs attention".
5. **Review queue**: owner sees image and proposed entry side by side, edits if needed, approves (posts) or rejects. Bulk approve for high-confidence items.
6. **File away**: after approval the file is renamed `2026-11-22_PY_323447769421_23200.jpg` (date, type, bank reference, amount) and moved from Inbox to the right month folder.
7. **No duplicates**: the bank reference number is unique; a second screenshot of the same transfer is flagged, and the bank statement import (F4) matches on the same reference.

**Sticker note convention** (recommended so extraction is reliable): first line the period ("Nov 26 Week 3"), then one line per part as "Label amount" (Tailor Salary 17500, Market 5700), then "Final Payment" or "Total". For owner funding: "Funds added OmerF 31700". Keep using the same words; the label list is editable in settings (e.g. Salary, Market, Advance, Fabric, Lace, Dye, Courier, Funds added).

## 9. Financial reporting framework and IFRS compliance

The owner requires the books to meet IFRS. The module therefore records every transaction using IFRS recognition and measurement rules and produces a complete set of IFRS-style financial statements. This section is a requirement for every phase, not a separate feature.

### 9.1 Which framework applies in Pakistan

Under the Companies Act 2017 (Third Schedule) the statutory framework depends on company size. A setting `fin_settings.reporting_framework` records which one Koko uses; recognition in the ledger is the same for all three, only presentation and a few options differ.

| Company size (Third Schedule) | Required framework | May opt for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small-sized: paid-up capital up to Rs 10 million, turnover up to Rs 100 million, up to 250 employees | Revised AFRS for SSEs (ICAP) | IFRS for SMEs or full IFRS |
| Medium-sized | IFRS for SMEs | Full IFRS |
| Large / public interest / listed | Full IFRS as adopted in Pakistan |  |

> Koko Atelier (Private) Limited very likely falls in the small-sized class today. Default setting: **IFRS for SMEs**, which is permitted for a small company, closer to full IFRS than AFRS for SSEs, and easy to switch. The accountant confirms the final choice; the system supports all three (Section 9.4).

### 9.2 Recognition and measurement rules (apply in all frameworks)

| Standard | Requirement | How the module implements it | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IFRS 15 Revenue (SMEs s23) | Revenue when control of the outfit passes to the customer, at the price after discounts. Advances are contract liabilities | `delivered_on` drives revenue; advances in 2200; order and reseller discounts reduce revenue (4800). Paid status records cash only | F3 |
| IFRS 15 Consignment and principal / agent | Goods with a reseller stay Koko inventory until the reseller sells. ### 9.3 Financial statements produced

All statements show the current period and a comparative, use the FS line mapping on accounts, and export to PDF (company header "Koko Atelier (Private) Limited") and XLSX.

- **Statement of financial position** with current and non-current classification (IAS 1 / SMEs s4).
- **Statement of profit or loss** (and other comprehensive income under full IFRS), expenses analysed by function (cost of sales, selling and distribution, administrative, finance cost) with a by-nature view available.
- **Statement of changes in equity**: share capital, retained earnings, profit for the year, prior-period adjustments, dividends.
- **Statement of cash flows** (IAS 7 / SMEs s7), indirect method, built from the cash-flow class on each account, with a direct-method view of bank and cash receipts and payments.
- **Notes pack**: accounting policies text, PPE movement schedule, inventory breakdown and NRV write-downs, receivables ageing and ECL, payables, contract liabilities (advances) movement, related party transactions, revenue by channel and by geography (Pakistan / UAE).
- Management reports (order and collection profit) stay separate and are labelled as management information.

### 10.1 Migration 013: ledger core

```
-- 013: finance core. Double-entry ledger: accounts, fiscal periods, journal entries and lines.
CREATE TABLE fin_accounts (
  id          SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  code        TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE CHECK (code ~ '^[0-9]{4}(-[0-9]{2})?$'),
  name        TEXT NOT NULL,
  type        TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (type IN ('asset','liability','equity','income','cogs','expense','tax')),
  parent_id   INTEGER REFERENCES fin_accounts(id),
  system_key  TEXT UNIQUE,                 -- e.g. bank_baf, ar_customers; code never hard-coded
  is_control  BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT false,  -- system posts only
  currency    CHAR(3) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'PKR',
  is_active   BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT true,
  created_at  TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT now()
);

-- fin_settings also holds tax_enabled BOOLEAN DEFAULT false and tax_enabled_from DATE (see Section 8.0),
-- reporting_framework ('AFRS_SSE','IFRS_SMES','IFRS') DEFAULT 'IFRS_SMES' and accounting_policies TEXT (Section 9).
-- fin_accounts also holds fs_line TEXT, is_current BOOLEAN, cash_flow_class TEXT
--   CHECK (cash_flow_class IN ('operating','investing','financing','cash')).
-- fin_periods includes a 13th adjustment period per fiscal year (is_adjustment BOOLEAN).
CREATE TABLE fin_periods (
  id          SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  fiscal_year TEXT NOT NULL,               -- '2026-27'
  starts_on   DATE NOT NULL, ends_on DATE NOT NULL,
  status      TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'Open' CHECK (status IN ('Open','Closed','Locked')),
  closed_by   INTEGER REFERENCES users(id), closed_at TIMESTAMPTZ,
  UNIQUE (starts_on)
);

CREATE TABLE fin_entries (
  id            BIGSERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  entry_no      TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE,      -- JV-2627-000123, SI-2627-000045 ...
  entry_date    DATE NOT NULL,
  period_id     INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES fin_periods(id),
  memo          TEXT,
  source_type   TEXT,                      -- order, customer_payment, reseller_item, expense, payroll ...
  source_id     TEXT,
  source_event  TEXT,                      -- delivered, cogs, settled, reversed ...
  status        TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'Posted' CHECK (status IN ('Draft','Posted','Reversed')),
  reversal_of   BIGINT REFERENCES fin_entries(id),
  created_by    INTEGER REFERENCES users(id),
  posted_at     TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT now(),
  created_at    TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT now()
);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX fin_entries_source_key ON fin_entries (source_type, source_id, source_event)
  WHERE source_type IS NOT NULL AND status <> 'Reversed';

CREATE TABLE fin_lines (
  id            BIGSERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  entry_id      BIGINT NOT NULL REFERENCES fin_entries(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  account_id    INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES fin_accounts(id),
  debit         NUMERIC(14,2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (debit  >= 0),
  credit        NUMERIC(14,2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (credit >= 0),
  currency      CHAR(3) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'PKR',
  amount_fc     NUMERIC(14,2),             -- foreign amount when currency <> PKR
  fx_rate       NUMERIC(14,6),
  party_type    TEXT CHECK (party_type IN ('customer','reseller','supplier','staff','director','courier')),
  party_id      TEXT,
  order_id      UUID REFERENCES orders(id),
  product_id    INTEGER REFERENCES products(id),
  collection_id INTEGER REFERENCES collections(id),
  consignment_item_id UUID REFERENCES consignment_items(id),
  tax_code      TEXT,
  description   TEXT,
  CHECK ((debit > 0) <> (credit > 0))
);
CREATE INDEX fin_lines_account_idx ON fin_lines (account_id, entry_id);
CREATE INDEX fin_lines_party_idx   ON fin_lines (party_type, party_id);
CREATE INDEX fin_lines_order_idx   ON fin_lines (order_id);
CREATE INDEX fin_lines_coll_idx    ON fin_lines (collection_id);

-- Deferred constraint trigger: a posted entry must balance; posted entries are immutable;
-- closed/locked periods reject new postings.
-- fin_settings: one-row table (go_live_date, revenue_event, sales_tax_registered, ntn, strn ...)
-- fin_fx_rates (rate_date, currency, rate_to_pkr)
-- Seed: chart of accounts (Section 5) and periods Jul 2026 - Jun 2027.
```

## 13. Go-live, data migration and testing

### 13.1 Go-live approach

1. Accountant provides the trial balance at 30 June 2026 (or last audited figures): cash, bank, receivables, stock at cost, fixed assets, payables, director balances, share capital, retained earnings.
2. Enter it with the Opening balances wizard (F0).
3. From F2 onward, record expenses and payroll from 1 July 2026. Omer has expense receipts for this period: F2 includes a **bulk receipt upload** (drop many photos/PDFs at once; each becomes a draft expense with the file attached, to be completed and approved from a review queue) plus an XLSX import template.
4. F3 backfill posts orders and reseller activity since 1 July 2026.
5. Stock at studio and with resellers is valued at standard cost once F5 is live; the difference to opening stock value posts to Costing variances with an owner-approved entry.

### 13.2 Testing

- Unit tests (node:test, no new dependency) for money helpers, every posting rule, the Trax import rules, matching engine and tax calculators.
- A seed script that builds a small fake month (5 orders, 1 reseller statement, 1 payroll run, 10 expenses, 1 bank statement) and asserts Trial Balance totals, P&amp;L and Balance Sheet figures.
- Manual acceptance per phase using the "Done when" list.

### 13.3 Non-functional requirements

- **Security**: finance routes require finance permissions; files only served to authorised users; CNIC and bank details masked in lists.
- **Audit**: every create / post / reverse / approve / close is in `audit_log` with before/after values.
- **Performance**: reports are SQL aggregations with indexes on `(account_id, entry_id)` and tag columns; consider a materialised monthly balance table only if needed.
- **Rounding**: tax computed per line and rounded to 2 decimals; rounding differences under Rs 1 to a rounding account.
- **Backups**: monthly XLSX export of the full journal to Google Drive (manual button first).
- **Mobile**: expenses, claims, payments and the overview must work well on a phone.
- **Language**: UI in English; amounts formatted `Rs 1,234,567` with PKR; AED shown as `AED 1,234.00`.
