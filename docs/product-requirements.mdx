## 1. Summary

Koko Atelier is a Pakistani women's and men's fashion label (pret, festive and luxury wear: kurtas, anarkalis, co-ords, lehengas, men's kurta sets) that sells through its own studio, WhatsApp, Instagram, the Shopify website kokoatelier.com, and on consignment through resellers abroad (Fashion Collage, Dubai). Outfits for retail customers are **stitched to order**.

Koko Atelier CRM is the single system the studio runs on. It replaced manual data entry in Notion and spreadsheets and now covers:

1. **Retail orders**: capture, measurements, stitching status, payments, delivery and receipts.
2. **Customers**: one profile per customer with order history and lifetime value.
3. **Catalogue**: collections and products synced from the Shopify website.
4. **Studio inventory**: shoot samples, reseller returns, spares and pieces made for a shipment.
5. **Resellers (consignment)**: handovers, stock at the store, monthly sales statements, payments in AED, barcodes, a view-only reseller portal.
6. **Finance**: a real double-entry ledger with IFRS-based statements, expenses, payroll, receipt intake and automatic posting of sales and reseller trade.

The product is moving from "Koko's internal tool" to a product in its own right. This PRD records what exists, what is required, and the direction (Section 11).

## 2. Problem statement

Before the CRM, Koko ran on a Notion database, WhatsApp threads, bank-app screenshots and Excel sheets exchanged with the reseller. The owners could not answer basic questions quickly or reliably:

- Which orders are due this week, and what stage is each in?
- How much is each customer still owed, and what advance has been taken?
- Which pieces are at Fashion Collage, which sold, which were paid for, and at what AED rate?
- What did the studio spend this month, and on what?
- Is the business profitable, by month, by collection, by design?

Data entry was duplicated (Notion + Shopify + Excel), error-prone, and nothing tied money to orders.

## 3. Goals and non-goals

### Goals

| # | Goal | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | One place for every order from every channel | 100% of orders entered in the CRM; Notion retired (done Nov 2026) |
| G2 | Fast order capture at the studio and on the phone | New order in under 2 minutes, measurements included |
| G3 | Always-correct balances | Advance = sum of recorded payments; balance due visible on every order |
| G4 | Transparent reseller trade | Collage statement, payments and stock reconcile to the piece; reseller sees only their own data |
| G5 | Every rupee recorded with proof | Expense from a phone in under a minute, receipt attached |
| G6 | Real accounts | Trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flows from posted entries; month close |
| G7 | Know the cost of a design | Cost per piece and margin per design and collection (Phase F5) |
| G8 | Tax-ready | Pakistan tax reporting switchable from 1 July 2027 (Phase F7) |

### Non-goals (for now)

- No customer-facing storefront or checkout (Shopify remains the shop).
- No writing back to Shopify (read-only integration).
- No automatic Shopify order sync: the owner reviews each website order before importing it.
- No payroll for per-piece external tailors (they are not paid by Koko; only the studio's commission is recorded).
- No multi-company tenancy yet (see Section 11).

## 6. Business rules (must hold everywhere)

1. **Stitch to order.** Retail orders never consume stock unless a line is explicitly filled from a studio piece.
2. **Revenue counts when the order is Paid** (owner decision, finance spec C1). Until then, money received is a customer advance.
3. **PR orders are free.** Amount and advance are always 0; never revenue; their items' prices are the PR value given.
4. **Cancelled and Returned orders** have no balance due and never count as revenue. An advance on them is refunded (default) or kept by an owner with a reason.
5. **The advance is the sum of payments.** Changing it on the order screens records a payment row automatically.
6. **Reseller commission is fixed per handover**, not per current reseller rate.
7. **Reseller money in AED** is converted at the sale-date rate; the payment's rupees are what reached the bank; the difference is an exchange gain or loss.
8. **Barcodes are assigned only once a reseller approves a shipment**; returned designs never get one.
9. **Posted accounting entries never change**; corrections are reversals. Closed months accept postings only from an owner with a reason; locked months accept none.
10. **Finance is visible only to owners and the Finance Manager.**
11. **Resellers see only their own data**, and only what they sold and paid.
12. **Dates are Pakistan dates** (Asia/Karachi); money is PKR unless stated.

## 7. Non-functional requirements

| Area | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Availability | Hosted on Vercel and Neon; no planned downtime for releases (push to `master` deploys) |
| Performance | Lists and dashboards load in under 2 s on a phone over 4G; a 12-month P&L for 5,000 lines under 2 s |
| Mobile | Every screen works at 390 px wide; dialogs become bottom sheets; key flows (new order, expense quick-add) are thumb-friendly |
| Security | Session cookie httpOnly, Secure in production; passwords hashed with scrypt; permission check on every API route; reseller data scoped in SQL |
| Privacy | CNICs never shown in lists or audit logs; from bank screenshots only names and the last 4 digits of accounts are stored |
| Integrity | Accounting rules enforced in Postgres triggers, not only in code; migrations run once and are recorded |
| Auditability | Every write audited; finance entries numbered per fiscal year (`JV-2627-000001`) |
| Data safety | Tests that post journals run only on the finance development database; live data is fingerprinted before and after every migration |
| Accessibility | Readable contrast in light and dark themes; keyboard access to dialogs and the command palette (Ctrl+K) |
| Localisation | English UI; Pakistani number and date formats; product names may be Urdu with English meaning |

## 9. Assumptions and dependencies

- Shopify remains the website; the CRM reads from it (public storefront JSON and a read-only Dev Dashboard app).
- Neon Postgres and Vercel remain the platform (see [TRD](./technical-requirements.mdx)).
- Claude vision reading of receipts requires an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` on the server.
- Bank: Bank Alfalah current account (PKR). Couriers: Trax (COD) and Uber (local).
- Fashion Collage sends a monthly sales sheet and pays in AED.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Paid dates before Nov 2026 are estimated from delivery / order dates | Flagged "estimated" on the order; owner reviews before year end |
| Payment methods of migrated payments are assumed (bank) | Marked "(check)" on the order until confirmed |
| Shopify rate-limits the public catalogue endpoints | Per-collection fetch only when needed; admin API fallback |
| Files stored in the database grow the database | 2.5 MB limit per file; move to Google Drive (Workspace Shared Drive) later |
| Single-reseller assumptions | Reseller features are already per reseller id; test with a second reseller before onboarding one |
| Owner-only knowledge | Help guides in the app; these product documents |

## 11. Product direction: from Koko's tool to a product

The CRM is built around one atelier's real workflow, which is its strength. To become a product other ateliers could use, these are the decisions and changes needed. **These are proposals for the owner to decide, not commitments.**

| Area | Today | Needed for a multi-brand product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tenancy | One company, one database | A `company` (tenant) key on every table, or one database per customer |
| Branding | Koko logo, wine and champagne palette, "Koko Atelier" on PDFs | Brand settings per tenant (logo, colours, receipt header) on top of the design tokens |
| Integrations | Koko's Shopify store, Trax pickup address, Bank Alfalah | Per-tenant settings for store, courier account and bank accounts |
| Finance | Chart of accounts and staff seeded for Koko | Template chart of accounts; onboarding wizard |
| Onboarding | Done by hand with scripts | Self-serve sign-up, import from CSV / Shopify |
| Billing | None | Subscription plans |
| Support | Owner and Claude Code | Help centre, in-app guides (exist for resellers and team) |

Recommended order: finish the finance phases that Koko needs (F4, F5), remove Koko-specific constants into settings (store, courier pickup id, bank, labels), then decide on tenancy.
